Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information PARTHU.AI ("PARTHU", "we", "us") collects through parthuai.com, our ads and our WhatsApp conversations, why we collect it, and the choices you have. PARTHU is run by Mahendra Singh Shekhawat in India and serves coaching institutes in India and the UAE.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you message us on WhatsApp or book a demo, we receive your name, phone number and anything you choose to share, such as your institute's name, city, the exams you teach and the number of students.
  • Website usage. When you visit parthuai.com, we collect information about your visit: pages viewed, how far you scroll, buttons you click (for example "WhatsApp us"), the ad or link that brought you, your approximate location (city or country), and your device and browser type.
  • Ad interactions. If you reach us through a Facebook or Instagram ad, Meta tells us which ad you interacted with.

2. Cookies and similar tools

Our website uses two tools that set cookies or similar identifiers:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC): helps us understand how visitors use the site.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, Inc.): measures how our Facebook and Instagram ads perform and lets us show our ads to people who have visited our site.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, and control ads based on your activity in your Meta ad preferences.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to you, schedule and run demos, and send quotes and invoices you ask for.
  • To understand which ads and pages work, and to improve our website and marketing.
  • To show PARTHU ads to people who visited our site or who are similar to people interested in PARTHU.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run PARTHU, and only as needed: Meta (Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp), Google (Analytics and Workspace), and our website host (GitHub Pages). These providers may process data outside India or the UAE under their own privacy and security commitments. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and demo details for as long as we are in conversation with you, and for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a customer (then we keep what the customer relationship and the law require). Website analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

6. Your rights and choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, at any time. You can also withdraw any consent you have given. To do this, message us on WhatsApp (details below). We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in India, these rights are provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you are in the UAE, they are provided under Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including limiting access to our business accounts to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you about a breach where the law requires it.

8. Children

Our website and ads are meant for coaching institute owners and staff. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the effective date at the top.

10. Contact and grievances
