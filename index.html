    Questions institutes ask

    What if the handwriting is very poor?

    We need clear photos and readable writing. If an answer can’t be read, it can’t be marked reliably, so we flag it and ask for a clearer photo.

    Are copies in Hindi or other languages checked?

    Right now we check descriptive copies written in English. Support for Hindi and other languages is coming later.

    How soon do we get the checked copies back?

    Usually the same day, and within 24 hours once we have your marking scheme.

    Which classes, subjects and exams are covered?

    Class 6 to 12 in all descriptive, long-answer subjects, plus CA Foundation, CA Intermediate, CA Final, UPSC and State PCS. Long answers in the style of UPSC Mains are covered too.

    Can we use our own marking scheme?

    Yes. Your teachers can share their own marking scheme. If you don’t have one, we can create it for you.

    How should we send the photos?

    Clear, full-page photos, one photo per page. You can also send all the pages as a single PDF.

    What if a mark looks wrong?

    Your teacher can override any mark.

    What does it cost?

    Pricing starts at ₹9 per copy + GST. Your exact quote depends on your classes, subjects and monthly volume.

    Is student data safe? Can your team read the copies?

    Student data is protected by design. The system is built so that neither our team nor any employee can read your students’ copies.
